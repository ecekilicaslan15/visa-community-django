Privacy

What this site stores, and what it doesn't.

  • Your account stores a username, an email address, an optional first and last name, and a hashed password.
  • Posts, replies, helpful marks, appointment opening dates, and reports you send are stored with your account.
  • Password reset messages are sent to the email on your account.
  • Deleting your account deletes that user and the posts tied to it.
  • We don't sell this data. VisaCommunity is a community project, not a visa agency.
