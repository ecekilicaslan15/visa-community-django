Guidelines

A few rules so the stories here stay useful.

  • Write about your own application. Don't invent an outcome.
  • Leave out passport numbers, application IDs, and anyone else's private documents.
  • No spam, insults, or advertising.
  • Questions are welcome. You don't have to finish the visa process to post.
  • Use Report on someone else's post if it breaks these rules. Staff can remove it.
💡
Tip — Charts marked “Community data” come from posts here. They are not an official decision about your application.
