Sponsorship letter

Sponsorluk dilekçesi, or taahhütname: a letter in which someone else — usually a parent, a spouse, or an employer — says they will pay for the applicant’s trip.

{% include "partials/guide_nav.html" %}
💡
Requirements differ per consulate and change over time — always check the current checklist of the consulate / visa centre you apply to. This is general guidance, not legal advice.

When it is needed

Use it when the applicant has no income, or not enough income, to cover the trip. That is common for students, homemakers, retirees, and minors.

What it must contain

  • The sponsor’s full name, ID number, address, and a phone number or email.
  • The relationship to the applicant.
  • The applicant’s name and a short description (student, spouse, employee).
  • The travel dates and the destination.
  • A clear statement that the sponsor covers all costs: travel, accommodation, living expenses, and insurance.
  • The date and the sponsor’s signature.

Documents usually attached

  • The sponsor’s recent bank statements.
  • Proof of income: payslips, an SGK service statement, a tax plate for a business owner, or a pension statement for a retiree.
  • Proof of the relationship, such as a multi-person civil registry record.
  • A copy of the sponsor’s ID.

Some consulates ask for a notarised letter. A minor also needs a notarised parental consent. Check the checklist for the consulate you apply to.

Parent sponsoring a student
