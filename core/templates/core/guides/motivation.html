Guides

Motivation letter

Niyet mektubu, or cover letter: a signed letter to the consulate. It says who you are, why you travel, what the plan is, how you pay, and why you will return.

{% include "partials/guide_nav.html" %}
💡
Requirements differ per consulate and change over time — always check the current checklist of the consulate / visa centre you apply to. This is general guidance, not legal advice.

What to include

Keep it to one page. Stay factual, and make every date match the bookings and the application form.

Header

Your full name, address, phone, and email, then the date, then the consulate you are writing to.

Do
  • Use the same address as on the form.
  • Name the consulate and the city.
Don't
  • Leave off the date.
  • Write to “the embassy” with no country.

Subject line

Use this line: “Application for a short-stay Schengen visa”.

Do
  • Keep the subject to that one sentence.
Don't
  • Add a slogan or a plea in the subject.

Purpose of the trip

Say why you are going, in one or two plain sentences.

Do
  • Name tourism, a family visit, study, or a named fair.
Don't
  • Write only “for tourism” with no plan.

Dates and itinerary

Give the exact travel dates and where you will be on which days. The main destination is the country where you stay the longest. If two stays are equal, it is the country where you enter the Schengen area first.

Do
  • Match the flights and the hotel nights.
Don't
  • Change a date between the letter and the form.

Accommodation

Name where you will sleep: hotel, or a host’s address if someone is inviting you.

Do
  • Give the name and city of each stay.
Don't
  • Leave a night unaccounted for.

Financing

Say who pays: you, or a named sponsor. Point to the bank statements or the sponsorship letter.

Do
  • Use the same amounts as the statements.
Don't
  • Claim savings the statements do not show.

Ties to Türkiye and the return plan

Job or school, family, and property if you have them, plus the day you go back to work or class.

Do
  • Name the employer or the university.
Don't
  • Promise you will “definitely return” with no fact behind it.

Enclosed documents

List what is in the file, in the same order as the stack.

Do
  • Include only documents you actually attach.
Don't
  • List a paper that is not in the envelope.

Signature

Sign by hand if the consulate asks for a wet signature. An unsigned letter is incomplete.

Do
  • Sign the same name as the passport.
Don't
  • Send the letter without a signature.

Common mistakes

  • Copy-paste templates that still contain someone else’s city or dates.
  • Dates that do not match the bookings.
  • A vague purpose.
  • An unsigned letter.
  • Too much emotion, and too few facts.
  • A letter that contradicts the application form.

Return flight reservation 6. Travel insurance Yours faithfully, Ayşe Yılmaz
