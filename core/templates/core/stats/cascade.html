The cascade rule

How a lawfully used short-stay visa can lead to a longer multiple-entry visa for Turkish nationals applying in Türkiye.

{% include "partials/stats_nav.html" %}

Commission Implementing Decision C(2025) 4694 of 15 July 2025 applies from that date. It covers Turkish nationals who live in Türkiye and apply in Türkiye for a short-stay visa. It does not cover truck drivers applying in their professional capacity.

Each step asks for a lawfully used previous visa — no overstay or misuse — and an application inside a set time after that visa expires. The visa can never run longer than the passport: if the passport ends sooner, the visa stops three months before the passport does.

Consulates keep their discretion. They can still refuse, shorten the visa in an individual case, or issue a long visa when the case justifies it. It is not automatic.
